# Code of Conduct

This repository is a working research and engineering surface. The quality of the
conversation matters as much as the quality of the code.

## Expected conduct

- Be respectful in technical disagreement.
- Critique the work directly and clearly without belittling the person doing it.
- Leave room for careful questions, partial understanding, and revision.
- Name risks, evidence gaps, and harms plainly when they matter.
- Credit prior work and avoid passing off borrowed ideas as your own.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, intimidation, or demeaning language
- Repeated bad-faith disruption
- Publishing private or identifying information without consent
- Sexualized language or hostile personal attacks
- Deliberately obscuring evidence, tests, or safety concerns

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, review comments, and any
other project spaces tied to the repository.

## Maintainer response

Maintainers may remove comments, close threads, or block participation when a
pattern makes the space harder to use well. When possible, moderation will be
brief, direct, and tied to the conduct rather than to the person.

## Reporting

For conduct concerns that should not be raised in public, use the security and
maintainer contact path described in [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md).
